# Commercial use

Lane's source is public under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE).

## What is free

Personal use and hobby projects are free under PolyForm NC. You can
build it, modify it, run it, share patches with friends. No paperwork.

## What is not free

Using Lane as part of a product you sell, wrapping it in a paid
service, or any other commercial use needs a separate commercial
license from Andy Hayes. PolyForm NC does not permit that.

If you are not sure whether your use counts as commercial, email
andy@boundlessitsystems.com and ask.

## Dual license

The community version is PolyForm NC. The paid version is a commercial
license, with source available to customers on terms we work out
together.

Pricing is not set yet. If you want to use Lane commercially, email
andy@boundlessitsystems.com and we will figure it out.
